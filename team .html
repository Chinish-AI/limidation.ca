The Craftsmen of Limidation砺地匠人

Everything has its limits; true craftsmanship is required. 万事皆有度，亦须真功夫

"Limidation" (砺地) speaks to pushing past the limits of space, and carving out a solid foundation beneath it. We bring geek-level rigor to every job, and use uncompromising craftsmanship to reshape the value of space. "砺地"（Limidation），意在突破空间的界限，雕琢坚实的基底。我们以极客般的严谨对待每一次施工，用极致工艺重塑空间的价值。

Limidation doesn't chase being "the best" — there's always more to learn, and beyond every mountain lies another; that kind of comparison never ends. What we pursue instead is making every single step, within the limits we've set for ourselves, as solid as bedrock. 砺地不追求做到"最好"——学无止境，山外有山，这样的比较本身就没有尽头。我们追求的是，在自己认定的限度之内，把每一道工序做得坚如磐石。

What you see on the surface is the finish — but what truly determines quality is the substrate work and structural foundation hidden beneath it. Leveling, waterproofing, tile-setting, grouting: every step is done without shortcuts or guesswork, built to withstand time and scrutiny alike. 表面看的是美观，真正决定品质的是看不见的基层处理与结构根基。找平、防水、铺贴、勾缝，每一步都不糊弄、不侥幸，做到经得起时间、经得起检验。

We are a professional construction team rooted in Victoria, BC. We don't push for limitless speed — only for doing the work to its fullest, within the bounds of our craft. At Limidation, every craftsman knows exactly where their line is: standards that must be kept are never loosened, and steps that must be completed are never shortchanged. What truly earns a client's trust isn't how good we say we are — it's that every floor we hand over stands there, solid, unmoving. 我们是一支扎根维多利亚的专业施工团队，不追求无限度地赶工，只追求在专业边界内把工作做到极致。在砺地，每一位匠人都清楚自己的边界在哪里——该守住的标准绝不松动，该完成的工序绝不打折。真正让客户放心的，不是我们说自己做得多好，而是每一次交付的地面，都稳稳地立在那里，坚固，不动摇。
