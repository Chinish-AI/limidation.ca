The Story Behind Our Name名字的由来

Limidation is a name formed from limit and foundation. Limidation 由 limit（限度）与 foundation（根基）组合而成。

Our Chinese name, "砺地" (Lai Dei), pairs "砺" — honed craftsmanship, tempered over years of hands-on work — with "地" — the plain, steady ground beneath our feet, built to last. 中文名"砺地"——"砺"取自"磨砺"，是手艺人经年累月打磨出的真功夫；"地"是脚下最朴实的根基，四平八稳，经得起时间考验。

Our Philosophy我们的理念

All things have their limits, yet true mastery still must be earned. 万事皆有度，亦须真功夫

Tile work is judged by its surface, but its quality is decided by what you can't see — the leveling, waterproofing, and base preparation underneath. We don't chase shortcuts or endless compromises. We work within sound professional judgment, and get every step right — that's our commitment to every client, and to the craft itself. 瓷砖铺贴，表面看的是美观，真正决定品质的是看不见的找平、防水与基层处理。我们不追求无限度地赶工或迎合，只在自己的专业判断范围内，把每一道工序做到位——这是我们对客户、也是对这门手艺的承诺。

Years of hands-on tile, renovation, and construction experience, based in Greater Victoria, BC — serving residential and commercial projects alike. 多年瓷砖、装修与建筑经验，扎根维多利亚（Greater Victoria），服务住宅与商业项目。
